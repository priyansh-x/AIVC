# Harmonic.ai

Key points:
- Sells the "sourcing layer" as SaaS — not running its own fund.
- Coverage of pre-incorporation founders is the differentiator vs PitchBook/Crunchbase.
- Scout agent: market mapping → IC prep, end-to-end research workflow.
- Customer list (a16z etc.) implies even the top platform VCs outsource the data layer rather than build it.
- Implies: a defensible AIVC wedge is probably NOT "better sourcing data" — that's commodified.
