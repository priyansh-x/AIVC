# Sajith Pai — "Reflections on a year in venture capital" (Blume)

Direct quotes / data points:

- "We typically get around ~3.5k pitches a year. About a sixth to a fifth of these are referrals."
- "The cold pitches get anywhere from a minute to five minutes (very rarely, a bit more) while the referrals get at least 15–20 mins and a mail back."
- "I don't think I have worked under 60 hours in any week since I joined Blume."
- "A day spent in meetings is at least 60–70 mails piled up."

Day shape: founder pitches, portfolio check-ins, LP meetings, journalist calls, hiring conversations, mentoring calls, fundraising support. Software stack: not mentioned by name in this piece — the work is described as human-intensive, not tool-mediated.

Implication for AIVC: the screening funnel at a typical Indian fund is roughly 3,500-pitches → small-percent meetings → handful of investments. Cold-pitch triage at 1–5 minutes per pitch is the most compressible layer in the workflow.
