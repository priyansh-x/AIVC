# LLM/agent capability limits (2026)

Key points:
- Hallucination is asymptotic, not solved. 8.2% average baseline; tails far worse.
- "Stateless reasoning" — chain-of-thought is post-hoc rationalization, not faithful introspection.
- Tool hallucination: agents invent or misuse tools, breaking long-horizon autonomy.
- Implication: full-autonomy investment decisions (commit capital based on agent output) are not safe in 2026. Human-in-loop at the *commitment* step is mandatory. AI can compress diligence; it cannot bear conviction.
