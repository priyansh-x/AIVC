# Tracxn

The dominant Indian-built sourcing/data platform; the closest thing the India market has to Crunchbase + PitchBook.

- Coverage: 7.1M+ companies globally, 678K+ Indian startups in DB (34.1K funded), 850+ VC/PE/IB customers.
- Architecture: AI signals + HITL editors. Founders (Neha & Abhishek Goyal) ex-Accel; IPO'd on BSE/NSE in 2022.
- User complaint (Capterra/SoftwareAdvice reviews): pricing "on the higher side in India context," no monthly plan, which prices out smaller/new Indian funds.

For AIVC: Tracxn is the *data layer* of Indian VC, not the *workflow layer*. It is not a competitor to associate-stack collapse; it could be a data feeder. The fact that smaller Indian funds find it expensive is a market signal — a pricing or workflow wrapper around publicly-available structured Indian-startup data (MCA/ROC + Tracxn-like signals) has a credible value-prop.
