# Power law in VC returns

Key points:
- Outliers, not averages, drive returns. Pattern recognition for the 4–6% is the entire game.
- Implication: AI optimization of the "middle of the funnel" (memos, screening) yields diminishing returns unless it changes the *tail* of picks.
- A quant model that picks "good companies on average" (Correlation's claim) does not necessarily pick fund-returning outliers — open question whether AI sees the 0.4% better than a senior partner with strong pattern recognition.
