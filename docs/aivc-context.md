# AIVC standing context

What every sub-agent and collaborator should know before starting work. Keep this short and current.

## Wedge

**AIVC leads with associate-stack collapse.** The screening + memo drafting + market research + comp-pull workflow currently done by junior investment staff. Of the 12 atomic VC activities in our [Phase 0 decomposition](../research/problem/operating-model-decomposition.md), this is the one most transformative for AI in 2026 and the most operationally proven path.

Locked: 2026-05-31. Rationale: [`research/briefs/2026-05-31-foundations.md`](../research/briefs/2026-05-31-foundations.md).

Portfolio-support transformation and contrarian-taste-augmented sourcing are **later** wedges, not abandoned. Sequencing intuition: associate-stack collapse first proves the brain on real workflow; portfolio support next requires more engineering depth; contrarian taste requires brand maturity.

## Geographic focus

**India first.** We have proprietary data on 3000+ Indian startups (see "Assets" below), which makes India our natural starting market. The Indian VC landscape is structurally different from the US — different active funds, different deal-flow patterns, different LP base, different valuation norms.

Default Indian-first when sizing, mapping competitors, or selecting interview targets. Global expansion is a later question.

Indian VC landscape to prioritize in research:
- Funds: Peak XV (ex-Sequoia India), Accel India, Blume, Elevation, Stellaris, 100x.VC, Together Fund, Pi Ventures, Lightspeed India, Nexus, Kalaari, Matrix India, 3one4, A91, Better Capital
- VC-tech / data layer in India: Tracxn (Indian-built, global), VCCEdge, Inc42, YourStory, plus any new India-built AI-for-VC players
