# Phase 0 — Foundations: what an AI-native VC firm actually is

## TL;DR
- "AI is the investor" is a marketing position, not an operating one in 2026 — even the most AI-native fund in market (Agent Fund) keeps humans on commit, because LLM hallucination (8–52% across benchmarks) and fiduciary accountability foreclose full autonomy this decade. ([[ai-native-vc-first-principles]])
- The economics genuinely break at two atomic activities: **screening + memo drafting** (collapses the associate-stack headcount line) and **portfolio support** (a16z's 150+ platform can plausibly be ~30% replicated in software). These are AIVC's two real wedge candidates. ([[operating-model-decomposition]])
- **CEO decision:** pick which wedge AIVC leads with — associate-stack collapse (changes our cost structure), portfolio-support transformation (changes how founders feel us), or contrarian-taste-augmented sourcing (highest-upside, highest-risk). The Phase 1 work plan branches off this call.

## Key findings (max 5)
1. **Of 12 atomic VC activities, only 2 are transformative for AI in 2026; 4 are meaningful; 6 are cosmetic or unmoved.** The transformative pair is screening/memo work and portfolio support. The unmoved set includes IC, network-gated sourcing, LP fundraising, and contrarian taste. ([[operating-model-decomposition]])
2. **Correlation Ventures has been "AI-native" for 15+ years and plateaued at ~$500M AUM, co-invest-only.** A workflow moat without sourcing access or brand caps the fund's scale. AI alone is not a fundraising story. ([[correlation-ventures]])
3. **SignalFire raised $1B in April 2025 on a 12-year proprietary-AI narrative — but the moat is the labeled-diligence corpus, not the AI itself.** A new firm cannot replicate that on day one. AIVC needs a wedge that doesn't require 10 years of priors. ([[signalfire]])
4. **58% of VC deals come from professional-network referrals; only 10% from cold inbound** ([HBS funnel data](../../brain/sources/2026-05-31-vc-funnel-metrics-hbs.md)). The majority of a fund's deal flow is gated on human relationship capital — which AI cannot manufacture. Sourcing-data tools (Harmonic etc.) are already commodified for top firms; "better data" is not a moat.
5. **Solo-GP-with-AI is the proven 2026 form factor at small-to-mid AUM** (Air Street $232M, Belief $20M, Sarah Smith $16M). Not yet proven >$300M without a strong brand instrument (Benaich's State-of-AI report, Gil's reputation). ([[air-street-capital]])

## So what
For AIVC, the decomposition forces three real choices: (a) where on the spectrum we sit — almost certainly Position 3 ("AI-native quant") with elements of Position 4 (agentic), not Position 5 ("AI is the investor"); (b) which atomic activity is our wedge; and (c) what our brand instrument is, given that AI alone is not a fundraising story. The decision the CEO has to make now is (b): which of the two transformative activities leads. This determines Phase 1's customer-discovery target (associate-stack pain → talk to fund operators; portfolio support → talk to portfolio CEOs at small/mid funds).

## Open questions
- Which wedge does AIVC lead with — associate-stack collapse, portfolio support, or contrarian-taste-augmentation? (CEO call. Drives Phase 1.)
- What's our brand instrument analog to Air Street's "State of AI" report — i.e. the content surface that converts authority into sourcing flow + LP credibility?
- AUM ceiling target: $25M Fund I (Vela), $50–100M (typical solo-GP), or aim for the $200M+ Air Street ceiling? Form factor diverges sharply across these.
