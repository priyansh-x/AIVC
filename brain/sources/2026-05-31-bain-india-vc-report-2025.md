# Bain India VC Report 2025

Top-line market shape:

- Deal volume: 880 → 1,270 deals (+45% YoY).
- ~95% of deals are <$50M ticket (seed through Series A/B). This is the relevant universe for AIVC's wedge.
- $50M+ deals nearly doubled, back to pre-pandemic levels.
- Fundraising itself: ~35% decline to $2.7B (lowest since 2020). But **maiden funds = ~1/3 of capital raised** — first-time funds in India are still emerging, including the structural opportunity AIVC fits into.
- Family-office + CVC participation roughly doubled YoY.

Implications: the market is bottoming-and-recovering on deals. ~1,270 deals × the screening-funnel constant (50–200× ratio) implies 60,000–250,000 pitches/year are processed across the Indian VC market. This is the total addressable workload AIVC's tooling/wedge could touch.
