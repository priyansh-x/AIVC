# Investment memo workflow

Key points:
- Memo drafting is the highest-volume analyst time-sink — 80% of associate time.
- Structure is highly templated → very AI-amenable.
- BUT: the political function of the memo (partner conviction signaling, partnership debate fuel) is irreducibly social.
- AI wedge: draft generation + auto-update of "market" / "competition" / "financials" sections. Partner-authored "why we win" and "risks I'm carrying" sections stay human.
