# VC funnel + time-allocation metrics

Key claims:
- Per-partner annual funnel: 500–1,000 sourced → ~200 meetings → 30–50 diligence → 8–12 invests.
- Firm-level: ~200 screens : 4 invests (HBS); ~101:1 ratio (alternative source).
- 58% of deals from professional network/referrals; only 10% from cold inbound.
- 22 of 55 weekly hours spent sourcing/networking.
- 118 hrs avg per deal in diligence, ~10 reference calls per deal (Gompers et al.).

Implication for AI leverage:
- The 500→200 triage is the highest-volume, lowest-judgment step — biggest cosmetic-to-meaningful AI lift.
- The 58% referral statistic suggests "more sources" doesn't unlock the relationship-gated deals — relationship capital is the bottleneck.
