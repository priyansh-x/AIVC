# EQT — Motherbrain

Key points:
- Monitors 10M+ companies across structured/unstructured sources.
- Combines external data (social, hiring) with EQT's proprietary historical diligence records — that internal corpus is a moat.
- Has sourced ~15 deals; one (Peakon, acquired by Workday) paid for the rest.
- Now extended across EQT (PE + VC) for portfolio ops: sales efficiency, market expansion.
- Pioneered the "AI + human expertise" framing — AI surfaces, partners decide.
