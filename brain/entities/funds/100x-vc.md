# 100X.VC

Sanjay Mehta's first-cheque India seed fund. Cohort-based; ~₹25L average cheque; invests through iSAFE notes. Has backed 109 founders POC → GTM as of recent disclosures.

Funnel signal: one published cohort processed **4,785 pitches → 458 shortlisted → 10 funded** (Class 05). That's a ~1.05% close rate and a ~9.6% short-list rate — implies an extreme pre-screening workload concentrated in cohorts. This is the *single most acute* candidate-buyer profile for screening-focused AI tooling in the Indian market: the throughput is real, the team is small, and the iSAFE structure means turnaround speed matters more than memo polish.

Relevance to AIVC: strong target-customer candidate for the screening sub-wedge specifically (less so for memo drafting, since iSAFE rounds skip the heavy IC memo).
