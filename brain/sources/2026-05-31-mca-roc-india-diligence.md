# MCA / ROC India — structured public-record layer

The Indian government's MCA portal is the canonical structured-data source for any registered company in India (AOC-4, MGT-7, ADT-1, charges, directors, financials). Probe42 and Credhive sit on top of it as cleanup/wrapper layers.

Why this matters for AIVC: any associate-stack tool serving Indian VCs has access to a *richer* public-record layer than US peers (where SEC filings only cover public + late-stage). MCA/ROC + our 3000+ private dataset is a structurally Indian moat that US-built tools like Affinity/Harmonic do not exploit by default.
