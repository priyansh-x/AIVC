# Vela Partners

- Explicitly positions as "AI-native" (vs "AI-augmented") — AI as infrastructure, not bolted on.
- Proprietary stack scores founder probability before product, market before traction.
- Speed wedge: 2–7 day soft commits.
- Small check sizes ($100K–$500K) suggest seed/pre-seed; volume play implied.
- Test case for the thesis that "tech improves capital allocation" — open question whether returns validate.
