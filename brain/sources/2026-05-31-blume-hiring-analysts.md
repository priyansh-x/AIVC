# Blume Ventures — analyst hiring commentary

Blume describes the analyst role as: scouting → dealflow → founder meetings → industry reports → trend analysis → portfolio support. Notes there is "no real playbook" — work organisation is individual-hustle-driven, not tool-mediated.

For AIVC: confirms that even at a top-tier Indian fund, the associate workflow is *not* systematised. There's no proprietary tooling layer between the analyst and the work. This is the gap. Tooling penetration is low precisely because nobody has built a workflow-aware product that fits the Indian associate role.
