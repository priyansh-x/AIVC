# India VC analyst/associate compensation — multi-source

Ranges (all per-annum, ₹ lakhs):

- **Tier-1 (Peak XV, Accel India, Lightspeed India):** ₹40–70L all-in.
- **Mid-size funds:** ₹20–40L.
- **Small / boutique:** ₹10–25L.
- **Averages:** Analyst ~₹22.9L; Associate ~₹30.2L (Glassdoor + PayScale).
- **Accel India pre-MBA analyst:** ~₹42L fixed (Levels.fyi).

Fully-loaded cost (incl. ESOPs, benefits, overhead): ~1.4–1.6× base, so a tier-1 associate effectively costs the fund ~₹70L–1.1Cr ($85K–$130K) annually.

For AIVC: 3–5 associates at a tier-1 Indian fund ≈ ₹3–5 Cr ($350K–$600K) /yr fully loaded. This is the cost line the "associate-stack collapse" wedge directly attacks. Note: smaller and mid-sized Indian funds operate with 1–2 associates max, so the *absolute* dollar replacement is smaller in India than in the US — but the *relative* share of fund opex is higher because Indian fund sizes are smaller.
