# Indian VC Survey 2025 — Taghash × Datum

Sample: 50+ Indian VC firms. Period: Q1 2025.

- **AI-first orientation:** 74% of surveyed VCs prioritise AI-first startups (investment thesis, not internal tooling).
- **Sentiment:** 68% expect improved fundraising in 2025.
- **Constraints:** 68% cite limited exit optionality as the dominant bottleneck; 45% cite LP-formation difficulty.
- **Deployment:** $3.2B in Q1 2025; secondaries/strategic >20% of capital deployed.
- **Bar-raising:** Series A ARR thresholds moved from ~$1M to ~$5M in many sectors — meaning more screening pre-Series A, longer holding patterns at seed, more diligence per cheque.
- The survey *does not* break out time allocation by activity. We do not have a clean Indian analog to the HBS "55 hr/wk partner" decomposition.

Implication: the bar-raise at Series A pushes more analytical workload upstream into seed-stage diligence — exactly the work AI can compress.
