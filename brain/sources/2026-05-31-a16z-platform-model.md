# a16z — Platform model

Key points:
- 150+ person operating platform — recruiting, marketing, policy, technical.
- This is THE counter-model to AI-native: leverage = humans, not software.
- Implication for AIVC: if you can replicate even 30% of a16z platform value with AI agents, the unit economics flip.
- Portfolio support is the most labor-intensive activity and likely the highest AI-leverage one outside sourcing.
