# Evertrace — VC dealflow at scale

Cross-checking source for workflow shape (non-India but ecosystem-standard):

- CRM dominance: Affinity, Attio at early stage.
- Signal layer (Evertrace, Harmonic) feeds CRM via integrations + MCP servers.
- Weekly pipeline review is the standard cadence.
- Dominant failure mode: "the CRM nobody uses" — adoption, not technology, is the moat.
- Pre-raise founder detection requires explicit pipeline stages or the relationship goes stale.

Note: Evertrace targets Europe + global, not specifically India. Cited as ecosystem benchmark.
