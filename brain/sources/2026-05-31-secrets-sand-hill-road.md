# Secrets of Sand Hill Road — Kupor

Key points used:
- VCs invest one company per category (anti-conflict rule) — limits diversification, raises stakes per pick.
- Partners are strategic backers, not just check-writers: recruiting, customer intros, board work.
- Decision process is partnership-driven (Monday meeting, IC vote) — slow by design, optimized for conviction not speed.
- This baseline informs what "irreducibly human" looks like: board judgment, partner-to-partner trust signaling, conflict adjudication.
