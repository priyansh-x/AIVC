# Tracxn

India-built (Bengaluru, 2013), public on BSE/NSE since 2022. Founded by Neha and Abhishek Goyal (ex-Accel). World's #2 private-markets data platform by volume after CB Insights: 7.1M+ companies, 850+ VC/PE/IB customers, 678K+ Indian startups in DB (34.1K funded). Architecture: AI signals + human-in-the-loop editors.

Reviews flag pricing as "high in India context, no monthly option" — prices out smaller and newer Indian funds.

Relevance to AIVC: data layer, not workflow layer. Not a competitor for the associate-stack wedge directly, but a benchmark of "what publicly-purchasable Indian startup data looks like" — and the pricing complaint signals room below Tracxn for a cheaper, more workflow-integrated alternative. Our 3000+ private dataset is *narrower* than Tracxn's 678K but is private-by-default — useful as a moat only if paired with workflow.
