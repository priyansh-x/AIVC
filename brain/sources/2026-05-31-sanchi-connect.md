# Sanchi Connect

Vertical deeptech sourcing layer for India. Used by deeptech VCs as a curated, pre-validated dealflow pipe; used by corporates for vetted POC partners.

For AIVC: orthogonal to associate-stack collapse — Sanchi solves *deeptech sourcing curation*, not associate workflow. But proves the market accepts vertical-curated sourcing rails in India.
