# EQT Ventures (Motherbrain)

EQT's bet: the proprietary diligence history of a 30-year PE shop is the moat. Public-signal aggregation is commodified; the firm-specific labels (what we passed on, why) are not. Motherbrain runs as an internal product team inside EQT, not a separate firm.

Relevance to AIVC: Motherbrain is the strongest argument that "AI eats VC" plays out *inside* incumbents first, not as a greenfield disruptor. AIVC's counter-thesis has to explain why a new firm beats a 10-year head start of labeled data.
