# Correlation Ventures

Original (2010) "Moneyball for VC" firm. ~$500M AUM, 371 investments. Pure co-investor — they ride someone else's diligence and lead.

Two important lessons for AIVC:
1. The quant-only model **works** when the firm doesn't have to source or lead — i.e. AI is doing portfolio construction, not founder discovery or conviction.
2. After 15+ years, Correlation has not grown into a category-defining firm. "Algorithmic VC" survived; it did not eat VC. The CEO should treat this as evidence that "pure AI investor" without relationship capital plateaus at modest AUM.
