# Reference calls / backchannels

Key points:
- 10 ref calls per deal, 118 hrs total diligence (Gompers et al.).
- The *backchannel* is where the real signal is — references the founder didn't pick.
- AI can prep questions, summarize calls, do sentiment analysis on transcripts. AI cannot trigger candor — that comes from "I know your old boss too" trust.
- Implication: AI compresses prep + synthesis. The call itself stays human, and the network access to get the call stays human.
