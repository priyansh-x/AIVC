# AI memo-drafting workflow — Claude / Fellow / Bessemer benchmark

Cross-source pattern for how AI memo drafting actually lands:

- Diligence calls captured by structured AI meeting assistants (Fellow, Otter, Granola).
- Transcripts + summaries fed to long-context LLM (Claude leading).
- LLM produces first-draft memo with quotes, themes, contradictions across calls.
- Human analyst sharpens, adds judgment, ships to IC.

Benchmarked savings (Bessemer Venture Partners, US): ~234 hours per analyst annually after AI integration. Affinity industry survey 2025: 85% of VC dealmakers use AI for daily-task automation.

India-specific gap: no published benchmark exists for Indian fund AI penetration in memo-drafting workflows. Anecdotally (IndianVCs.com tech-stack catalogue), Claude is named as the leading memo-drafting tool; absolute adoption rates are not measured. This is a real research gap for AIVC.
