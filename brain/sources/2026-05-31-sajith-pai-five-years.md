# Sajith Pai — "Reflections on completing five years in VC"

Key extracts:

- Workflow decomposes into **Finding** (sourcing/screening/investing) and **Minding** (post-investment support). "At least two-thirds of the day doesn't change structurally" between junior and senior roles — meetings, calls, email dominate.
- **Friction-as-strategy** for inbound: 50+ Alfred email snippets, default 30-min Zoom (not 1-hr in-person), templated rejection language designed to convert "2/3 of requests into non-meetings."
- Stack: Vimcal, Apple Notes, Gmail, Alfred. No CRM mentioned by name.
- Investment criteria evolved from "Team > TAM" → "Traction > Team > TAM" as fund sizes grew and entry valuations rose.
- Pain points: continuous WhatsApp notifications across all hours; long feedback cycles (18–24 months to see whether a call was right); "Heisenberg problem" — the more diligence you do, the more it changes the founder relationship; loss of deep-concentration capacity from context-switching.

For AIVC: confirms the Indian principal-level workflow is bottlenecked at inbound triage and at maintaining founder-rapport-while-doing-diligence — both candidates for AI-mediated relief.
