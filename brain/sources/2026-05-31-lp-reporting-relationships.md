# LP relationships + reporting

Key points:
- LP reporting is templated AND trust-laden — AI can do the data assembly, but the "honest discussion of risks" tone is what wins re-ups.
- Re-up economics: established LP base drops cost-to-raise by ~67%, time by 6–9 months — implies LP relationship capital is the second-most valuable asset of a fund (after track record).
- Fundraising is irreducibly human: pitching pension funds and endowments is a multi-meeting, multi-year sales cycle.
- AI wedge: data-room automation, KPI rollups, portfolio-narrative drafting. Not the relationship.
