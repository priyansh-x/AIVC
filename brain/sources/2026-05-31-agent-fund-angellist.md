# Agent Fund (AngelList rolling fund)

Key points:
- Furthest-out point on the AI-native spectrum currently in market: "semi-autonomous."
- Still explicitly keeps humans on the final commitment decision.
- Rolling-fund structure (quarterly LP subscriptions) is itself well-matched to high-frequency, AI-driven deployment.
- Implication: even the most AI-native fund in market does not claim AI commits capital. The "AI is the investor" position is still a marketing phrase, not an operating one (2026).
