# Founder traits + ML prediction

Key points:
- Personality is a measurable, predictive signal — and AI can extract it from public text.
- Conscientiousness + (low) neuroticism dominate Big-Five impact.
- 6 founder archetypes; diverse combos vastly outperform solo founders.
- Implication: founder evaluation, long treated as the irreducibly human "gut" call, has a meaningful AI-extractable component. Not a full replacement for intuition but a real wedge.
