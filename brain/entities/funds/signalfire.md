# SignalFire

Founded 2013 (Chris Farmer, Ilya Kirnos). $1B closed April 2025 on top of existing AUM.

The clearest example of the "tool-augmented platform VC" archetype scaled large. SignalFire bet early that proprietary data infrastructure would compound — and the $1B 2025 raise validates that LPs now believe the AI moat narrative even at scale.

Relevance to AIVC: SignalFire shows what the *augmented* end of the spectrum looks like with capital and time behind it. AIVC cannot out-data SignalFire on talent flow signals; it must pick a different wedge.
