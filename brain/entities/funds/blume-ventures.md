# Blume Ventures

India's most-quoted seed/Series-A fund. ~$900M AUM across multiple funds; Mumbai-headquartered with team across Bengaluru / Delhi-NCR. Co-founded by Karthik Reddy.

Operating shape (from Sajith Pai's public reflections, principal-level): ~3,500 pitches/year through the fund; cold pitches get 1–5 min, referrals 15–20 min; 60+ hour weeks; 60–70 emails accumulated per meeting-heavy day. No CRM named publicly by Pai; firm is a Taghash customer per Taghash's homepage.

Relevance to AIVC: archetypal target-customer profile *and* archetypal target-competitor profile depending on framing. A fund of this scale running a tools-light workflow is exactly the gap the associate-stack collapse wedge addresses — *but* their existing scale and brand mean they don't *need* AIVC's tools; they'd buy the workflow uplift or build it. The opportunity for AIVC is one tier below — funds without Blume's analyst depth.
